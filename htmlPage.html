The Velvet

A serpent glides, its secrets deep. It dances through the velvet night. Of ancient paths and legends old. A mystery wrapped, a life adored.

The Lion's Domain

In grass that sways like whispered flame, the lion waits—no need for name.

Whispers of the Dawn

Golden threads of morning light, Chasing away the quiet night. The sleeping world begins to wake, As songbirds sing for morning's sake.

Dancing in the Rain

Cool drops fall from slate-gray skies, A sudden storm to our surprise. Puddles sparkle, streetlamps gleam, The afternoon becomes a dream.
